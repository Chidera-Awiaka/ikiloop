Why “Ikiloop”?

Ikigai is four circles: what I love, what I'm good at, what people need, and what can pay. ikiloop is me going round them again and again. The loop is ∞, an 8 on its side, my life path number. Sumi is the ink. Ember is the spark, used sparingly. Ash is the grey between drafts. Washi is the blank page.
